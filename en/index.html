Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) about Relocation

Is it possible to rent a property without intermediaries for the long term?
Yes, the KeyHaven Asia platform allows you to rent property directly from verified owners. You save on agency commissions, while all contracts are guided by our experts for your safety.
Do you help with obtaining a residence permit?
When buying investment property in countries like Indonesia and Thailand, we provide consulting on legalization, investment visas, and residence permits (KITAS, Elite Visa, etc.).
How are utility bills paid?
In most condominium and apartment contracts (Vietnam, Thailand), water and electricity are paid according to government meters. In villas (Bali), pool and garden maintenance is often included. We specify these conditions in detail before check-in.
